# PRODUCT.md — Igor Luiz Portfolio

## Register
brand — portfolio site where design IS the product.

## Users & Purpose
- **Primary audience**: Small business owners, entrepreneurs, and professionals in Brazil looking for freelance development services
- **Context**: They visit from Instagram, WhatsApp referrals, or Google search. Mobile-first (60%+ traffic from phones)
- **Job to be done**: Quickly understand what Igor offers, see proof of quality, and get in touch

## Brand Personality
- **Tone**: Confident, direct, technical but approachable. No corporate fluff
- **Visual identity**: Dark premium (#0a0a0c), gold accents (#c8a45a), glass effects. Inspired by luxury tech, not SaaS templates
- **Voice**: First person, casual Brazilian Portuguese. "Eu construo" not "Nós oferecemos"

## Anti-References
- Generic WordPress agency sites
- Fiverr/99designs freelancer profiles
- Corporate landing pages with stock photos
- Neon-on-black "hacker" aesthetic
- SaaS cream/pastel templates

## Strategic Principles
1. **Show, don't tell** — Interactive demos beat static descriptions
2. **Speed to contact** — Every section should funnel toward WhatsApp/email
3. **Mobile-first** — Most visitors are on phones
4. **Proof over promises** — Animated counters, live demos, real projects
5. **Distinctive** — Must look different from every other dev portfolio in Brazil
